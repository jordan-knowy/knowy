IMPORTANT PRODUCT STRATEGIC PIVOT — COMPLETE UX/UI REPOSITIONING

We are pivoting the product architecture.

The previous direction was:
- too SaaS-heavy,
- too dashboard-centric,
- too broad,
- too enterprise-complex for MVP,
- too many features,
- too much organizational intelligence too early.

We want to radically simplify the product.

====================================================
NEW PRODUCT POSITIONING
====================================================

KNOWY is now:

“An AI meeting preparation intelligence layer.”

The core product value is:
Knowy automatically prepares every important meeting using:
- email context,
- calendar context,
- relationship history,
- public information,
- company intelligence.

The goal is NOT:
- to replace CRMs,
- to become a massive analytics dashboard,
- to become an internal operating system immediately.

The goal is:
TO CREATE A “MAGICAL” MEETING PREPARATION EXPERIENCE.

====================================================
MOST IMPORTANT PRODUCT PRINCIPLE
====================================================

The product must do ONE thing exceptionally well:

“Understand the room before the meeting starts.”

Everything else is secondary.

====================================================
CORE UX STRATEGY
====================================================

The product should feel:
- invisible,
- fluid,
- contextual,
- lightweight,
- intelligent,
- effortless.

The product should NOT feel:
- like heavy enterprise software,
- like a CRM,
- like a surveillance tool,
- like a dashboard overload,
- like a psychology platform.

====================================================
NEW PRODUCT STRUCTURE
====================================================

The MVP is now composed of ONLY:

1. Gmail / Outlook connection
2. Calendar intelligence
3. Automatic meeting detection
4. AI-generated meeting briefs
5. Lightweight dashboard
6. Lightweight Chrome/Safari extension
7. Relationship memory layer

REMOVE OR DEPRIORITIZE:
- complex team dashboards
- advanced admin analytics
- complex org mapping
- full CRM replacement logic
- live meeting AI assistant
- complex graphs
- advanced revenue intelligence
- organizational operating system logic

Those are V2 or V3.

====================================================
NEW UX PHILOSOPHY
====================================================

The UX must optimize:
- speed,
- clarity,
- focus,
- contextual intelligence,
- emotional trust.

The user should feel:
“Knowy quietly prepares me better than anyone else.”

====================================================
MOST IMPORTANT USER MOMENT
====================================================

The most important moment in the product is:

Receiving a beautifully designed AI-generated meeting brief before a meeting.

THIS IS THE CORE EXPERIENCE.

Everything should revolve around this.
